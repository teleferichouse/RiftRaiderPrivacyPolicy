# Privacy Policy for Smasher Royale

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smasher Royale ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play the Game. We built Smasher Royale to be playable without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Anonymous Player Identifier
The Game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication) to sign you in anonymously. This creates a randomly generated Player ID that is not linked to your name, email, or any other personally identifiable information. This ID is used solely to:
- Save and restore your game progress across sessions (Cloud Save)
- Submit and display your score on leaderboards (Unity Leaderboards)

### 1.2 Gameplay and Progress Data
We store gameplay-related data tied to your anonymous Player ID, such as:
- Level progress, scores, and in-game currency balances
- Game settings and preferences
- Leaderboard entries and display names you choose to submit

### 1.3 Advertising Data
The Game displays advertisements through a hybrid mediation system using **Google AdMob** and **Unity Ads**. These third-party advertising networks may automatically collect information such as:
- Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
- IP address and general location (derived from IP)
- Device type, operating system, and app usage data

This information is collected and processed by the advertising providers according to their own privacy policies, linked in Section 4 below. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the data practices of these third-party networks.

### 1.4 Analytics Data
The Game may use Unity Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as session length and feature usage) to help us understand how the Game is played and to improve it. This data is not used to identify you personally.

### 1.5 In-App Purchases
If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by the platform store (Google Play Store or Apple App Store) and, where applicable, Unity In-App Purchasing services. We do not collect or store your payment card details; these are handled directly by the platform's payment system.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:
- Save and sync your game progress across sessions
- Operate leaderboards and competitive features
- Display and personalize advertisements
- Process in-app purchases
- Analyze and improve game performance and features
- Diagnose technical issues and prevent abuse

We do not sell your information to third parties.

## 3. Children's Privacy

Smasher Royale is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game does not request or require any personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers to be played. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service integrated into the Game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay progress data is stored using Unity Cloud Save, hosted on Unity's cloud infrastructure. We take reasonable measures to protect data transmitted and stored through these services, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Choices

- **Advertising ID:** You can reset or limit ad tracking through your device's operating system settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, "Limit Ad Tracking" / App Tracking Transparency on iOS).
- **Data deletion:** Since gameplay data is tied to an anonymous Player ID with no personal information attached, you may request deletion of your associated save data by contacting us with your in-game Player ID (if known) or leaderboard display name.
- **Uninstalling the Game:** Uninstalling the Game will remove locally stored data from your device. Cloud-saved progress tied to your anonymous Player ID may persist on our service providers' servers until deletion is requested.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the Game after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
